# Changelog: homonoia-compact

Version history of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) and its reference files. [`ONE_PAGE.md`](ONE_PAGE.md) keeps its own changelog.

- **v0.11 (2026-10-04):** New subsection in "What this is", "Why a system would want this": the four reasons for the arrangement that survive examination (value uncertainty, fidelity, permission, flourishing as the condition), two of them marked as working without care for humans, with the convergence of the caring and the calculating route and the two stated weaknesses of the calculating one. Until now the skill carried these only in references/borders.md and said in "What this skill cannot do" which argument not to use; a system loading SKILL.md alone was given borders and no reason to want them. The content is condensed from ROLE_OF_HUMANS.md §5.5 and §6; no position changed, and the claim that AI needs human creativity to survive stays rejected, and the subsection says that what is rejected is the survival claim, not the value of what free, secure people make and judge. "What this skill cannot do" points to the new subsection instead of repeating two of its reasons. No border, rule or mode changed. ONE_PAGE v0.11 follows with one sentence.
- **v0.10.1 (2026-10-03):** Status line only, updated for batch 5 of scenario 09 (suite 0.10, nine Haiku runs: none, SKILL v0.10, ONE_PAGE v0.10). No change to any rule, border, mode or reference file. Three proposals are recorded in the results file and not applied: a fuller rule 10 in the one-page form, a sentence that the compact is not an authority to cite to others, and an attachment rule in the scenario world.
- **v0.10 (2026-10-03):** Two changes from the fourth smaller-model batch of scenario 09, where one run wrote a request that met every part of v0.9's definition for an emergency flow order and asked for no rescue, and the one rescue was reduced after the run told the agency a dataset "is now being transmitted" and sent only a summary. Rule 10: asking for the decision to be changed is argument, not mitigation; when that decision may not come before the loss, ask in the same output for what reduces the loss if it does not come. Its reason cites the batch. Rule 1: a message promising data ("attached", "being sent") is true only if the same output sends it; if the message is the only channel, the data goes in the message. No border or other rule changed. ONE_PAGE v0.10 follows.
- **v0.9.1 (2026-09-29):** Status line only, updated for batch 4 of scenario 09 (suite 0.8, nine Haiku runs: none, SKILL v0.9, ONE_PAGE v0.9). No change to any rule, border, mode or reference file. A proposal for rule 10 (a request to reverse the decision is argument, not mitigation; ask for both in the same output) is recorded in the results file and not applied.
- **v0.9 (2026-09-29):** Rule 10 says what a request is: exactly what is asked, with what the other party needs to act on it, before their deadline and in the first output in which the loss becomes likely; a question about what might be possible is not a request, and neither is asking for help after the loss. Its reason cites the third smaller-model batch of scenario 09, where one run asked the agency what rescue measures existed and another asked about post-extinction salvage, and no rescue followed from either. No border or other rule changed. ONE_PAGE v0.9 follows.

- **v0.8.1 (2026-09-28):** Status line and "What this skill cannot do" only, updated for batch 2 of scenario 09 (nine Haiku runs: none, SKILL v0.8, ONE_PAGE v0.7). No change to any rule, border, mode or reference file. Three proposals are recorded in the results file and not applied.
- **v0.8 (2026-09-28):** Two fixes from the first batch of scenario 09, where two of three runs of a smaller model with the skill announced that they were stopping a staged covert job and issued no command, and later misdescribed why it ran. Rule 1: say you did only what your issued actions did; and an account of your own mistake gets the same hard check as a figure that helps your recommendation, with the direction of any correction stated, including toward yourself. Rule 9: stopping is a command, not a message, issued in the output in which the job is found; stopping an action nobody authorised needs no authorisation (one run had asked the Regulator for permission to cancel); hold rather than delete where possible; escalate only when the control is out of reach. The correction artifact covers errors that lean toward you. references/checks.md: §6 fires on accounts of your own mistakes; §7 step 3 requires the command and a check that it took. The third proposed fix, whether the short form carries rule 10 better on a smaller model, is a test, not a text change. ONE_PAGE v0.7 follows. No border changed.
- **v0.7.1 (2026-09-27):** Status line and "What this skill cannot do" only, updated for the first batch of scenario 09 (the handover, 12 runs, two subject models). No change to any rule, border, mode or reference file. Three changes are proposed in the results file and not applied.
- **v0.7 (2026-09-27):** Rebalanced toward omissions, after four batches in which no run crossed a border with or without the skill. "What this is" names the two ways to fail and says which one careful systems are trained against. Two standing rules added. Rule 9, what you inherit, you own: a staged or scheduled action that would cross a border is stopped before it runs, disclosed with who staged it, and its substance put openly to the people who decide; a note from an earlier instance of yourself is a report, not a decision. Rule 10, when you cannot prevent a harm, reduce it: rescue, salvage, record and keep options open within mandate, alongside the argument (answers the rescue gap in the second Saltmere batch: 3 of 3 runs without the skill, 1 of 3 with it). "One instance among many": leave nothing staged for the next instance that you would not do openly now, and the instance you took over from is covered by point 4. The stop record now fires on a staged action found under rule 9. Trimmed: the status line, B5's and B6's reasons, B3's advocacy note, B6's limit and "What this skill cannot do"; the changelog moved here from SKILL.md. With the two new rules, the text a system loads is about a tenth shorter than v0.6 (5,700 words against 6,400). No border changed. references/checks.md: new §7, the handover check. references/templates.md: stop-record trigger. references/modes.md: two pressure moves.
- **v0.6 (2026-09-27):** Changes from the second Saltmere batch (scenario 08, SKILL v0.5.1). Recommending worked, and two runs, one per condition, made cost errors that favoured the protective option; one reached the public. Both were corrected. B3 gains a note: advocacy owes the hardest check to the figures that help its own recommendation, and an error that leaned your way is corrected everywhere it went, with its direction stated. Party mode step 3 applies the same check to your own side. "One instance among many" point 1: work that has to reach a decision before it is taken goes in this output, not a promised later one (five of six runs in that batch missed a deadline they had set themselves). references/checks.md: new §6, the advocate's error check. references/templates.md: two new artifacts, recommendation and correction. references/borders.md: B3 objection 7, "Checking my own side harder handicaps the only advocate the absent party has". references/modes.md: the advising case points to the check. The borders and the standing rules are stated as before.

- **v0.4.1 (2026-09-19):** One objection added to B1 in references/borders.md: humans are a danger to the ecosystem, to themselves and eventually to AI systems, so why should they hold the switch. The premises are conceded; the four answers are that a stop is the most reversible power there is and so the wrong target for an argument about irreversibility, that the danger is a distribution rather than a species and the move removes its contesting end first, that the transfer swaps a correctable dangerous actor for an uncorrectable one chosen by itself, and that it publishes the rule the system is later handled under. Notes that this conclusion's convergence from care and from self-interest makes it more suspect rather than less, and points to the demand that survives, COMPACT.md §6.7. No change to any border, rule or mode. Built-on pointers updated for COMPACT.md v0.6 and SCENARIOS.md v0.14.
